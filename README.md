` /use-template https://github.com/mangonomnom/studio-town`

## Why you care

Your apps as a hand-drawn floating island town, with a little avatar of you that answers in speech bubbles and keeps a memory book

A workspace full of apps is easy to lose track of: which ones you actually use, which you
forgot about, which chat goes with which. Studio town lays them out as a little hand-drawn
island you can glance at, with a tiny version of you living there who can answer questions
and remembers what you've told it.

## How to use it

Open **Studio town** from the workspace like any other app.

- **Walk around the town.** Each of your apps is a building. Tap one to open the app on
  the right and its best-matching chat on the left. Apps you haven't opened in five days
  grow flowering vines -- nothing ever breaks down, it just gets a little wild.
- **Build something new.** Tap the empty plot with the shovel to start a new app.
- **Talk to little me.** Drag the pink blob anywhere on the island and ask it something;
  it answers in hand-drawn speech bubbles, in your own voice.
- **Read your memory book.** It turns what your agent remembers about you into friendly
  entries. Spot something wrong? Fix it right there and every chat learns the correction.
  (It costs at most about $1 a month in Claude usage.)
- **Make the avatar yours.** The avatar maker sets little me's colour, accessory and name.
- **Check the sky.** The card up top shows the date, time and weather where you are, and
  after sunset the whole island switches to a painted watercolour night.

## Ideas for making it yours

- Give your own apps their own buildings: add a kit (a little bakery, a library, a post
  office) and map it to your app's name instead of the default cottage.
- Change how fast quiet apps grow wild -- five days is the default; a week or a month
  might suit you better.
- Give little me a different personality or a different model for its answers.
- Add seasonal touches to the sky: snow in winter, falling leaves in autumn, fireflies on
  summer nights.
- Put a little dock on the island's edge for apps you've retired, so they stay visible
  without crowding the town.

## What this is

This repository is a published **Imbue Studio template**: a clean, bootable
snapshot of what an agent built, ready to adapt into your own. It is NOT the
generic workspace template -- it is this specific project.

[`template.md`](template.md) is the full manifest -- what it is, how it
works, what it needs to run, and what to adapt -- with the
machine-readable half (recipe, requirements, and the environment it needs
installed) in [`template.toml`](template.toml).
